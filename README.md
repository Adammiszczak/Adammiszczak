## 🧭 How I approach search

I start by understanding how a system works and what the available evidence supports. Then I connect the technical findings to the product, engineering and operational decisions that can address them.

For emerging AI Search features, I distinguish documented platform behavior from analysis and hypotheses. I focus on useful measurement and sound technical foundations rather than promises of rankings, citations or traffic.
